Palm Flag

How to play

  1. ★

    Two ways to win

    Bring the enemy flag back to your base, or knock out your rival with paintballs.

  2. ⚑

    Capture the flag

    Grab the flag from the other team's base and carry it home to win. Your own flag must be at home when you get there.

  3. ●

    Paintballs

    You have 100 health and each hit takes 20, so 5 hits is a knockout. Hit a flag carrier to send the flag home.

  4. !

    Watch the arena

    Walls block players and paintballs. The striped barriers slide back and forth, so time your runs.

  5. ⌨

    Controls

    ↑ ↓ ← → move · Space shoot · R play again after a match

  6. ⇄

    Teams

    Whoever creates the room plays Blue (left side). Whoever joins plays Red (right side).
